The short answer: With Spectora Pay at Close, the inspector is paid within 1-2 business days of publishing the report, even if the home sale never closes. Spectora collects from the buyer at closing. If the deal falls through, Spectora charges the backup card the buyer gave at booking, and the inspector keeps their payout.
