What is Pay at Close? Pay at Close lets home buyers defer their inspection payment until closing, while the inspector gets paid within 1–2 business days of publishing the report. Spectora takes on the collection risk and coordinates with the title company, escrow officer, or closing attorney, so the inspector never waits on closing day to get paid.
