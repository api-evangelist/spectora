Grant Blackwell is the owner of Candid Home Inspections in upstate South Carolina, and he used Spectora's API to build something new on top of a platform he already runs his business on: an automated system that turns raw thermal imaging photos into a branded, client-ready report inside Spectora. The result? A process that used to take real time on every inspection now takes minutes, and a business running more efficiently because of it.
