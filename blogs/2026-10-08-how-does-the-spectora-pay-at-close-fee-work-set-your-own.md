The short answer: Spectora Pay at Close lets homebuyers pay for their home inspection at closing, while the inspector is paid within 1-2 business days of publishing the report. Inspectors can set their own additional Pay at Close fee, up to $100, to offset the cost of providing the service or build real revenue.
